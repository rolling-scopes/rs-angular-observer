Conclusion for the mentor

Overall: A three-person team with a working deployed product and consistent diary coverage across all three sprints. The project is a credible crypto trading SPA with real Binance Testnet data, auth, routing, and now a reasonable set of directives and pipes. However, the process is informal: virtually no peer review happens through GitHub (all real merges go through sprint-2-checkpoint branches authored by pasha-learns), and the team lead has never attended a sprint interview. The quality distribution is uneven — Vitalina's sprint-3 work is the most technically sophisticated in the team, pasha-learns is a solid integrator with modest per-sprint feature output, and sashadrozdov94 contributes genuine but thin work in sprints 2–3.

Strengths:

  • All three students maintained a diary throughout — 3 entries each, all substantive and technically grounded.
  • Vitalina's sprint-3 work (token expiry with effect/untracked(), ErrorMessagePipe, ClickOutsideDirective, ENVIRONMENT token, toast) demonstrates genuine Angular depth well above the minimum.
  • The project is deployed on GitHub Pages with SPA routing configured correctly.
  • No signs of unattributed AI-generated content; all diary entries describe specific decisions and trade-offs in first person.

Watch:

  • pasha-learns has zero defence scores across two sprints. This is the primary flag. Even if scheduling explains sprint-1, sprint-2 has just closed and is also unscored.
  • No PR review culture. Work is merged by the lead without review. Students may not have experienced code review feedback as a learning signal.
  • sashadrozdov94-spec contributed ~2h in sprint 2 and ~2h in sprint 3. If the sprint-2 interview happens, he may struggle on routing/lazy-loading topics he did not personally implement.
  • The feature/markets-and-state branch name appears in the remote refs but its commits are already on main (the alias in the clone suggests a local naming artifact). Not a blocker, but the branch hygiene is loose.

Recommended interview angle: For pasha-learns, start by resolving the missing-score situation — then use the markets/trade flow he built to probe Signal-based data flow and lazy loading. For Vitalina, go straight to untracked() in her effect and the ClickOutsideDirective design — both are in her diary in detail; she should own them fluently. For sashadrozdov94, use sprint-1 material (signals, inputs, ApexCharts wiring) as the baseline, then probe lightly on routing and change detection since he touched both in sprint 3 via OnPush on DashboardComponent.
