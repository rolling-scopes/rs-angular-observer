Conclusion for the mentor

Overall: A three-person team with a live, deployable product, a genuinely ambitious scope (custom UI kit + commercetools), and the highest repo activity of any team reviewed at this snapshot. The codebase quality is high, enforced by elrouss's CI/lint stack and active code review culture. All three students have diary entries for every sprint, which is uncommon. However, the contribution split is stark: elrouss has 85 commits and 42 PRs; the other two combined have 23 commits and 17 PRs across three sprints.

Strengths:

  • elrouss is among the most technically capable and self-reflective students in the cohort. His diaries read as professional engineering retrospectives, not student journals. His carousel decision (no Swiper.js, built natively after mentor discussion) and UiService drawer coordination pattern show genuine architectural thinking.
  • prokhorovd has the sharpest Angular learning arc of the three: from CVA over-engineering in sprint 1 to correctly diagnosing catchError stream termination in sprint 3. His self-awareness about React-to-Angular migration is explicit and productive.
  • den987655 is consistent and present — three diary entries, one feature per sprint, real commercetools debugging in sprint 3. He is not carried; he is slower.

Watch:

  • elrouss's burnout disclosure in the sprint-3 diary is the most significant flag. He describes losing interest in the course. Whether this persists into sprint 4 is unknown, but the mentor should address it directly and privately, not just score it.
  • den987655's Cart page PR (#144) was open at snapshot. If it hasn't merged, the sprint-3 feature set is incomplete on main.
  • The interview-driven format that elrouss criticises ("time goes into interview prep instead of building") may shape his engagement during the defence itself — he may be less invested in it than earlier sprints.
  • Imbalance risk: if elrouss's engagement drops, the project delivery capacity drops sharply because prokhorovd and den987655 together are not producing enough volume to compensate.

Recommended interview angle: For elrouss, treat the interview as a peer conversation, not a knowledge check — his diary makes clear he has outgrown the Q&A format. Ask about architectural decisions and leadership retrospectives. For prokhorovd, the catchError/switchMap arc is the ideal probe — follow his own diary thread and ask him to explain rather than demonstrate. For den987655, stay inside his Cart page scope and test whether he understood the commercetools concepts he documented or worked through them by trial and error alone. The Feynman threshold: can he explain anonymous session flow, SKU, and version-based cart updates without code in front of him?
