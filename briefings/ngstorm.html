Conclusion for mentor

Overall readiness: Two students are ready to defend (iskonplus: strong; anast-ananko: solid). shchepadzmitry is unprepared unless there is activity not visible in the public repo.

Balance: The commit distribution (99 / 68 / 7) is problematic. iskonplus has driven the majority of the application — search, cart, product details, auth pages, profile tabs, animations, OnPush migration — across three sprints. anast-ananko owns auth/registration/profile domain clearly. shchepadzmitry's contributions end at the Swiper slider and three home-page sections in week 1–2.

Strengths:

  • iskonplus and anast-ananko both tackled the commercetools API honestly (OAuth2 flows, action arrays, token chaining) — this is real complexity, not tutorial work.
  • No evidence of wholesale AI copy-paste in either active student. iskonplus explicitly notes when AI is used (2 of 11 entries, for learning/conceptual questions). anast-ananko's diary shows genuine struggle and iterative reasoning.
  • The team adopted signals early and used them consistently (computed, signal, signal inputs) — not just boilerplate.
  • OnPush migration across all components is an atypical sprint-3 move for beginners.

Risks:

  • shchepadzmitry: 24 days of silence is a hard risk. Recommend the mentor open with a direct question on what he built in Sprint 2/3. If blank, the issue must be escalated.
  • The commercetools backend adds coupling: some Angular concepts (DI, services, state) may be entangled with API specifics in their mental model. Probe "what would you change if the backend were a plain REST API with simple JWT auth?"
  • Open issues include "Basket (Cart) Page Implementation" and "User Profile Page Implementation" — these appear to be tracking issues, not bugs, but 6 open issues at checkpoint time is worth a quick scan.

Recommended interview angle: Start iskonplus on OnPush and ViewChildren (he touched both last sprint). Start anast-ananko on the switchMap / operator-choice question and the ControlContainer directive. For shchepadzmitry: begin at the Swiper PR, confirm basic understanding, then quickly probe routing and lazy loading — the gap between his claimed work and the rest of the course content will be apparent within 5 minutes.
