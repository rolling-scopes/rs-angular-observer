Conclusion for the mentor

Overall: The strongest team configuration seen in this cohort's sprint 2 briefing set. Three students, three distinct domains, genuinely even workload, all active through the sprint 2 deadline. The product is ambitious — real commercetools API integration with auth, catalog filtering, cart (including promo codes), and full profile CRUD — and evidence across PRs and dev notes suggests each student built their domain rather than watched.

Strengths:

  • Nikita (Atikin132) is a credible team lead who runs the board, coordinates task distribution, authors sprint merges, and still does the heaviest individual feature work. His diary writing spans 7 entries with explicit design reasoning.
  • Artur (agtugchik) has the best diary discipline on the team (8 entries) and shows genuine diagnostic ability — the computed() + FormGroup issue and the provideAppInitializer pattern are well-understood.
  • Oleg (kostevoleg) demonstrates the most architectural maturity in his cart integration diary — the N-requests bug find, the InjectionToken for currency, the service split — all documented with clear reasoning, not just outcome.
  • The team runs proper GitFlow with branch naming convention, CommitLint, and weekly standups documented in meetings-notes/. Process maturity is high.

Watch:

  • Signals theory depth: the Sprint 2 interviewer flagged this for all three students. None of them describe the change detection / signal graph mechanics in their diaries — they use Signals effectively but may not be able to explain what happens when a signal inside a computed() updates while the component is detached.
  • Nikita's Signal Store knowledge is self-described as still forming ("потихоньку разбираюсь"). Given Signal Store is central to the catalog feature, probe it specifically.
  • Artur has not written custom validators yet — the Sprint 2 interviewer already flagged this. It is the natural next step; verify at interview.

Recommended interview angle: All three students scored 100/100 twice. The interview goal is to find the ceiling, not catch failures. Use diary-anchored specifics: take one problem they documented solving and ask them to explain it from first principles without referencing their code. For Nikita: commercetools attribute-scope debugging. For Artur: computed() + FormGroup incompatibility and reactive forms under the hood. For Oleg: cart versioning in commercetools (why does every update need a version?). If answers are fluent, escalate to signal internals: "What does Angular do when a signal inside a computed() changes — how does the framework know which components to re-render?"
