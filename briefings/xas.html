Mentor briefing — XAS

Cross-interview briefing · RS Angular 2026 Q2

Source: repository SlavikusVOG/XASon-online-store (commits, PRs, dev diaries across all branches). Snapshot 2026-06-24. Goal: give the mentor context in 2 minutes before the defence — who did what, who understands it, what to probe.

One-liner: Green — all three members active through the Sprint 2 deadline, substantive diaries for every sprint, and mentor scores of 95/90/100 (Sprint 2).
