  • Daniil (mikhalenkadaniil) is a credible lead — infra is solid, CI/CD is in place, code review overhead is real.
  • The team runs a proper GitFlow with PR reviews, branch naming conventions, and a Kanban board. Process maturity is above average for this course.

Watch:

  • Lorenzo's absence in sprint 2 is the main flag. Probe him first thing; if he can fluently explain his sprint-1 work, he may have had a personal sprint-2 obstacle. If he struggles with sprint-1 material, the problem runs deeper.

Recommended interview angle: For each student, take one thing they claim to have built and ask them to recreate or explain from scratch (the Feynman threshold). Alena: forkJoin chunking or canDeactivate guard. Daniil: resource on the Discover page or the CI/CD trigger chain. Lorenzo: httpResource in the About component or the Taiga dark-mode solution. Avoid generic Angular questions — use the diary trail to make questions specific.
